Policy: Confidential Data Sharing Policy
Version: v2
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. Confidential Information
Employees must protect confidential company information, including customer data, financial records, business plans, internal documents, and source code.

2. External Sharing
Employees must not share confidential information with external parties unless they have prior written approval from an authorized manager and the recipient is authorized to access the information.

3. Personal Accounts
Employees must not use personal email accounts or unauthorized file-sharing services to transfer confidential company information.

4. AI Tools and Public Platforms
Employees must not upload confidential company information to public AI tools or unapproved external platforms.

5. Security Incidents
Employees must promptly report suspected unauthorized disclosure of confidential information to the company's designated security team.
