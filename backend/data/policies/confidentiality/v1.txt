Policy: Confidential Data Sharing Policy
Version: v1
Effective Date: 2025-01-01

1. Confidential Information
Employees must protect confidential company information, including customer data, financial records, business plans, and internal documents.

2. External Sharing
Employees must not share confidential information with external parties unless they have prior written approval from an authorized manager.

3. Personal Accounts
Employees must not use personal email accounts or unauthorized file-sharing services to transfer confidential company information.
